## 01. Welcome to BestWord

Chapter: starting · Scene: welcome

Welcome to BestWord, a crossword game for two players. Build connected words, discover bridges, and earn the higher final score. Let's learn the rules and controls.

On screen:

- Two players · One shared board
- Build words. Connect letters. Score bridges.

## 02. Find your next game

Chapter: setup · Scene: join

Create an account or sign in. In the lobby, advertise a game or join another player's table. The host chooses five, fifteen, or twenty-five minutes per player.

On screen:

- 5, 15 or 25 minutes per player
- 30-second increment

## 03. A fresh crossword every game

Chapter: setup · Scene: setup-board

The fifteen by fifteen board has no bonus squares or blank tiles. Two distinct random words, each nine to twelve letters long, cross at one letter. They consume tiles but score nothing.

On screen:

- 15 × 15 · Columns A–O · Rows 1–15
- Two distinct opening words: 9–12 letters each
- Opening words use tiles, but score 0

## 04. Ready, set, play

Chapter: setup · Scene: setup-ready

Both players must connect before the three-second countdown. The first player is random. Racks begin empty. If someone never arrives, the unstarted game closes without a winner after twenty-five seconds.

On screen:

- Both players connect → 3-second countdown
- Random first player · Empty starting racks
- Pre-start no-show: cancelled after 25 seconds

## 05. Six vowels, including Y

Chapter: letters · Scene: vowels

A, E, I, O, U, and Y are vowels. Take vowels directly from the shared bag when placing them. Consonants come from your private rack, which holds at most ten letters.

On screen:

- Vowels: A E I O U Y
- Vowels come from the shared bag
- Consonants come from your private rack

## 06. Every turn starts with a draw

Chapter: letters · Scene: automatic-draw

At each turn's start, consonants arrive automatically. With eight or fewer on your rack, draw up to two. With nine, draw up to one. With ten, draw none. Bag availability limits every draw.

On screen:

- Rack 0–8 → draw up to 2
- Rack 9 → draw up to 1
- Rack 10 → draw 0
- Draw once, automatically, before choosing an action

## 08. Place at least two new tiles

Chapter: legality · Scene: legal-placement

Place at least two new tiles in one row or column, connecting to existing tiles. Together, old and new letters must form one continuous principal word, reading rightward or downward.

On screen:

- At least 2 NEW tiles
- One row or column; connected to the board
- Continuous principal word: left → right or top → bottom

## 09. Every crossing must work

Chapter: legality · Scene: secondary-words

Perpendicular words created by your new tiles are secondary words. Every principal and secondary word must contain three to fifteen letters and appear in BestWord's dictionary. Two-letter crosswords are invalid.

On screen:

- Principal = word in your move direction
- Secondary = perpendicular word made by a new tile
- Every word: 3–15 letters and in the dictionary

## 10. Principal words cannot repeat

Chapter: legality · Scene: word-history

Neither player may repeat a previous principal word, including either opening word. Extending one into a different word is allowed. Secondary words can repeat. New tiles may mix vowels and consonants freely.

On screen:

- No repeated principal words, including opening words
- Prefixes and suffixes may create a new word
- Secondary words may repeat · Any vowel/consonant mix

## 11. Click, choose direction, type

Chapter: input · Scene: directional-entry

Click an empty square: the right arrow starts horizontal entry. Each later empty-square click clears your draft and switches direction, even on a different square. Type available letters; the cursor skips existing tiles.

On screen:

- First click: ⇨ horizontal
- Next empty-square click: ⇩ vertical
- Type → cursor skips occupied squares

## 12. Edit your draft

Chapter: input · Scene: edit-entry

Backspace removes the most recently typed tile and returns the cursor there. Clicking an occupied square clears the draft without moving the cursor. Press Enter to submit the complete word for validation.

On screen:

- Backspace: remove the latest typed tile
- Occupied-square click: clear draft; keep cursor and direction
- Enter: submit

## 18. What makes a bridge?

Chapter: scoring · Scene: score-bridge-definition

Look at the board before your move. Within the completed word, the first and last existing tiles are pillars. Each previously empty square strictly between them is one span. One or more spans makes a bridge.

On screen:

- Pillars = first and last EXISTING tiles in the word
- Span = each previously empty square BETWEEN pillars
- One or more spans → bridge

## 24. Two formulas to remember

Chapter: scoring · Scene: score-recap

Principal: multiply the letter sum by the sum of consonants and spans. Secondary: double the letter sum for a bridge, otherwise count it once. Y is a vowel. All-vowel principals without spans score zero.

On screen:

- Principal = letter sum × (consonants + spans)
- Secondary = letter sum × (bridge ? 2 : 1)
- Y is a vowel · All-vowel principal with 0 spans scores 0
- Bridge scoring never overrides placement rules

## 25. NO WORDS skips just this turn

Chapter: actions · Scene: no-words

NO WORDS skips only this turn and scores zero. It is available only when you actually drew a consonant this turn and your opponent has not passed. Otherwise, place a word or permanently pass.

On screen:

- Skip this turn; play again later
- Requires at least 1 consonant drawn THIS turn
- Unavailable after the opponent has passed

## 26. PASS is permanent

Chapter: actions · Scene: permanent-pass

PASS gives up this turn and every future turn. Your score and clock freeze after the final increment, and you draw no more. You may leave. Your opponent continues taking consecutive normal turns.

On screen:

- PASS ends ALL your turns
- Final increment, then score and clock freeze
- Opponent continues · You may leave

## 27. How the game ends

Chapter: actions · Scene: ending

When both players pass, the higher total score wins. Equal totals are a draw. An empty bag does not end the game; use available tiles, or pass when you're finished.

On screen:

- Both PASS → compare scores
- Equal scores → draw
- Empty bag alone does not end the game

## 28. Watch your active clock

Chapter: clocks · Scene: clocks

Only the active player's clock ticks. Every completed turn adds thirty seconds, including NO WORDS and PASS. Running out of time loses immediately, regardless of score. Your move must reach the server before zero.

On screen:

- Only your own turn uses your time
- Completed turn → +30 seconds
- Time reaches 0 → loss, regardless of score

## 29. Reconnect promptly

Chapter: clocks · Scene: disconnect

After the server detects a player disconnection, that player has twenty-five seconds to return. Their active clock keeps running. Missing the deadline loses regardless of score. A player who already passed is exempt.

On screen:

- 25 seconds FROM SERVER DETECTION
- Active clock keeps running
- Passed players are exempt

## 30. Service interruptions pause play

Chapter: clocks · Scene: recovery

A confirmed service interruption pauses clocks and reconnect allowances. After recovery, remaining players have two minutes to return. Play resumes after a short countdown; if someone stays absent, the game closes without a winner.

On screen:

- Infrastructure pause: clocks and reconnect allowances stop
- After recovery: up to 120 seconds to return
- Resume countdown, or close without a winner
